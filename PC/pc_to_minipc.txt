Whenever you alter the physical IP layer framework, you must update the software parameters to point to the new addresses:Update the ROS Network Variables: Inside your Docker container startup scripts or profiles, you must change your ROS_IP definitions to match the new settings:Jetson container: export ROS_IP=192.168.10.10 [1]Mini PC container: export ROS_IP=192.168.10.20 [1]Update the Zenoh Bridge Execution Loops: When firing up the TCP tunnels, pass the newly updated listening parameters:Mini PC container: ./zenoh-bridge-dds --listen tcp/192.168.10.20:7447 [1]Jetson container: ./zenoh-bridge-dds --connect tcp/192.168.10.20:7447 [1]Verify via Ping: Always run a quick diagnostic check to make sure the network is active:

ping -c 4 192.168.10.20
